## New

- **First release.** BudSwitch moves your headphones between your Mac and your iPhone from the menu bar: **Mac** connects them, **iPhone** lets go of them so pressing play on your iPhone pulls them over, and ⌃⌥⌘B toggles between the two.
- **Lock and sleep.** Locking the Mac lets go of the headphones once nothing plays through them, and sleep always does.
- **Update notifications.** BudSwitch checks GitHub once a day and sends a notification when a new version is out.
